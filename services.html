Our Services

Pickup and Delivery

We pick up packages from homes, offices, stores, or business locations and deliver them safely to customers or designated destinations.

Same-Day Delivery

Need your package delivered urgently? Our same-day service gets items to customers quickly, subject to availability.

Interstate Waybill

Send packages and goods from Lagos to different parts of Nigeria through our dependable interstate waybill service.

Home Delivery

We make it easy for businesses and individuals to deliver packages directly to customers' homes.

Office and Corporate Delivery

Reliable delivery solutions for offices, organisations, and corporate clients.

E-Commerce Delivery

We support online vendors with dependable order delivery, including Payment on Delivery.

Payment on Delivery

We collect payment from customers on behalf of participating vendors. POD orders are remitted within 24 hours after successful delivery.
